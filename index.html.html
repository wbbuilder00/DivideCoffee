The Sourcing

Sourced with
intention.

Our coffee is specialty-grade. Ethiopian and Brazilian single-origin beans, chosen for their distinct character and traceable provenance. Nothing commodity. Nothing anonymous.

Our matcha is ceremonial grade — the highest classification in Japanese production. Stone-ground, shade-grown, and handled with care from harvest to cup.
